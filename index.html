What is soft stone

The texture of stone, made from natural clay.

Soft stone (MCM, modified clay material) is made from natural clay and recycled inorganic solids instead of quarried rock. It faithfully reproduces the look and feel of travertine, marble, slate, timber and woven fibre, in sheets that are ultra-thin, lightweight and flexible.

  1. 01Natural raw materialsNatural clay and inorganic solid waste are collected and sorted.
  2. 02Fine grindingThe materials are milled into a fine, even powder.
  3. 03ModificationA surface treatment links the particles into a layered, interwoven network that makes the material plastic.
  4. 04Flexible stone sheetThe modified clay is formed into thin sheets that recreate natural stone textures.
